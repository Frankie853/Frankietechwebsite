Frequently asked questions

Which locations make the most from a hotspot?

Places where many people gather and need to get online: market places, hotels and guest houses, residential areas, and bus stations. If your location is different, we will tell you honestly after a free site survey.

What equipment do you use?

We choose hardware after the site survey. Typically that is a MikroTik router as the controller, indoor or outdoor WiFi access points such as Tenda WiFi 6 units, Tenda F3 and F6 routers for smaller sites, Huawei XPON (GPON and EPON) fiber terminals, Ubiquiti LiteBeam M5 radios for long-distance links, and a power backup unit so the hotspot stays online during outages. If you already own equipment, we can review it during the survey.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the site and the cabling needed. After the site survey we give you a clear timeline before any work starts.

Can my customers pay by M-Pesa?

Yes. We set up a login page where customers buy access with vouchers or M-Pesa, and you can track sales and users from the management dashboard.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We monitor the network, manage users, bandwidth and vouchers, and troubleshoot problems. Call or message us on WhatsApp any time you need help.

Which areas do you cover?

Tell us where your site is through the contact form or by phone. We confirm whether we can serve it and arrange a site visit.
